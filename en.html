About Minghao

Biography

Dr. Minghao Dang has been selected for China’s national overseas high-level talent program and will join Zhongshan School of Medicine, Sun Yat-sen University (Guangzhou). He earned his bachelor’s degree in Bioinformatics from Huazhong University of Science and Technology and his PhD in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from the Institute of Biophysics, Chinese Academy of Sciences. He conducted computational biology research at Baylor College of Medicine and Duke University before joining MD Anderson Cancer Center in 2019 to work on cancer bioinformatics and single-cell multi-omics.
